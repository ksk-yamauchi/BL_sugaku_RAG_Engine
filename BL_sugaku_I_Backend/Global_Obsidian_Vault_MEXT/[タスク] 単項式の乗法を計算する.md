## 📝 関連する演習問題
```text
[問題] 次の計算をせよ。
$2a^2 \times (-3a^3)^2$
[解説] [正解] $18a^8$
[解説]
$2a^2 \times (-3a^3)^2 = 2a^2 \times (-3)^2 (a^3)^2 = 2a^2 \times 9a^6 = 18 \times a^{2+6} = 18a^8$
```
```text
[問題] 次の計算をせよ。
$(-2x^2 y^4)^3 \times 5x^2 y$
[解説] [正解] $-40x^8 y^{13}$
[解説]
$(-2x^2 y^4)^3 \times 5x^2 y = (-2)^3 (x^2)^3 (y^4)^3 \times 5x^2 y = -8x^6 y^{12} \times 5x^2 y = -40x^8 y^{13}$
```
```text
[問題] 次の計算をせよ。
$2x(x^2 - 3x)$
[解説] [正解] $2x^3 - 6x^2$
[解説]
$2x(x^2-3x) = 2x \cdot x^2 + 2x \cdot (-3x) = 2x^3 - 6x^2$
```
```text
[問題] 次の計算をせよ。
$(x^2 - 3xy - 2y) \times (-xy)$
[解説] [正解] $-x^3 y + 3x^2 y^2 + 2xy^2$
[解説]
$(x^2 - 3xy - 2y) \times (-xy) = x^2 \cdot (-xy) + (-3xy) \cdot (-xy) + (-2y) \cdot (-xy) = -x^3 y + 3x^2 y^2 + 2xy^2$
```
```text
[問題] $$(-2x^2)^3 \times x^4 = [コサ]x^{[シス]}$$
[解説] [正解] コサ：-8，シス：10
[解説]
$(-2x^2)^3 \times x^4 = (-2)^3 (x^2)^3 \times x^4 = -8 \times x^6 \times x^4 = -8x^{10}$
```
```text
[問題] $$3xy^3 \times (-4x^2 y)^2 = [セソ]x^{[タ]} y^{[チ]}$$
[解説] [正解] セソ：48，タ：5，チ：5
[解説]
$3xy^3 \times (-4x^2 y)^2 = 3xy^3 \times (-4)^2 (x^2)^2 \times y^2 = 3xy^3 \times 16x^4 y^2 = 48x^5 y^5$
```
```text
[問題] $$2x(3x^2 + 4x) = [ツ]x^3 + [テ]x^2$$
[解説] [正解] ツ：6，テ：8
[解説]
$2x(3x^2 + 4x) = 2x \times 3x^2 + 2x \times 4x = 6x^3 + 8x^2$
```
```text
[問題] $$(a^2 + 2ab - 3b) \times 3ab = 3a^3 b + [ト]a^2 b^2 - [ナ]ab^2$$
[解説] [正解] ト：6，ナ：9
[解説]
$(a^2 + 2ab - 3b) \times 3ab = a^2 \times 3ab + 2ab \times 3ab - 3b \times 3ab = 3a^3 b + 6a^2 b^2 - 9ab^2$
```
